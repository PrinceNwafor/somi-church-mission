Your First Step

We've Been Expecting You

Come experience the presence of God and a community that will feel like family from day one. Here is everything you need to know.

What to Expect

1
Arrive at Your Own Pace
Our doors open 30 minutes before service. There is no rush — come as you are. Our welcome team will be at the entrance to greet you warmly and help you find your seat.
2
Experience Powerful Worship
Our worship sessions are alive and anointed. Expect music that draws you into God's presence — this is not performance, it is an encounter.
3
Receive the Word
Prophet Stephen Ovat brings messages that unlock Kingdom mysteries and stir your supernatural consciousness. Come ready to receive revelation for your life.
4
Connect with the Family
After service, our team will connect you with resources, ministry groups, and opportunities to go deeper. You are not just a visitor — you are family.
