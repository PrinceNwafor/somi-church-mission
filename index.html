Our Spiritual Father

Meet Prophet
Stephen Ovat

Prophet Stephen Ovat is the Lead Pastor of The SOMI Church Mission — the HolyGhost Centre — located in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria. A well-known Nigerian minister and prophetic voice, he is renowned for his deep revelatory teachings on the mysteries of the Kingdom of God.

Prophet Ovat co-leads the church alongside his wife, Pastor Peggy Ovat. Together they have built a community that is passionately committed to encountering God through the Holy Spirit — through prophetic teaching, healing, deliverance, and authentic fellowship.

His messages are available on THE SOMI TV YouTube channel, Apple Podcasts, and Spotify, reaching thousands of believers around the world.
