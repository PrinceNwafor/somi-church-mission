Our Story

We Are The SOMI Church Mission

A Holy Ghost Centre birthed by the Spirit of God with a clear mandate to unveil the mysteries of the Kingdom of God to this generation.

Founded with Purpose

How It All Began

The SOMI Church Mission — also known as The SOMI Church HolyGhost Centre — was established in Lagos, Nigeria by Prophet Stephen Ovat under the direct leading of the Holy Spirit. The church was birthed with one burning vision: to raise a unique people who are fully conscious of the supernatural life available to every believer.

Rooted in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, The SOMI Church Mission has become a gathering place for those hungry for authentic encounters with God — through prophetic teaching, healing, deliverance, and Holy Spirit-led worship.

Today the church reaches thousands through its physical services and the THE SOMI TV digital platform on YouTube, Apple Podcasts, and Spotify — carrying the mandate of bringing Kingdom revelation to the ends of the earth.

Lead Pastor

Prophet Stephen Ovat

Founder & Lead Pastor — The SOMI Church Mission

Prophet Stephen Ovat is a well-known Nigerian minister and the Lead Pastor of The SOMI Church Mission (HolyGhost Centre) in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos. Known for his deep revelatory teachings on faith, the Holy Spirit, and the mysteries of the Kingdom of God, Prophet Ovat carries a distinctive prophetic grace that has transformed thousands of lives.

He co-leads the church alongside his wife, Pastor Peggy Ovat. Together they have built a Holy Ghost community passionate about encountering God and walking in supernatural dimensions of Kingdom life.

His sermons and teachings are broadcast on THE SOMI TV (YouTube), Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and Clubhouse — where thousands connect for daily prayer watches and prophetic releases at 6am, 9am, 12pm, and 3pm WAT.
