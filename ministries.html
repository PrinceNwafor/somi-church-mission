Our Ministries

Six Ways to Go Deeper

Whether you are hungry for community, prayer, outreach, or spiritual growth — there is a place for you here.

🤝

Connect Circles

Our small group community is the heartbeat of SOMI Church. Connect Circles are intimate gatherings where you build real friendships, study the Word together, and do life with others who share your faith journey.

Join a Circle
🌍

Outreach & Missions

We are called to serve beyond our four walls. Our Outreach team extends the love of God into the streets of Lagos and beyond — feeding the hungry, caring for the vulnerable, and sharing the Gospel with those who have not yet heard.

Get Involved
🔥

Glam & Fire

A dynamic youth and young adult community that burns with passion for God. Glam & Fire conferences and events are spaces where the next generation encounters the Holy Spirit, builds Kingdom relationships, and discovers their prophetic identity.

See Events
🎓

LITS Youth Ministry

LITS is our dedicated youth programme raising a generation that is rooted in God's Word and alive in the Holy Spirit. From Bible study sessions to mentorship and outreach — LITS is where young people find purpose and belonging.

Learn More
🎥

Media Ministry

THE SOMI TV is our multimedia arm — producing sermon recordings, live streams, podcasts, and digital content that carry the revelation of God's Word to the nations. Available on YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts.

Watch Now
🙏

Daily Prayer Watches

We believe in the power of corporate prayer. Our daily prayer watches run at 6am, 9am, 12pm, and 3pm on Clubhouse, with the 6pm evening watch held onsite at the church. Join us and experience the atmosphere of Heaven.
