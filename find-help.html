When You're Hurting

God Is Close to the Broken

Pain — whether physical, emotional, or relational — can make you feel like God is far away. But the Word of God says He is near. He does not waste your suffering. He redeems it.

"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds." — Psalm 147:3

Watch: Messages on Healing Submit a Prayer Request
When You Feel Overwhelmed

Cast Your Care on Him

Anxiety, worry, and mental heaviness are real. But you are not meant to carry the weight of the world. The Holy Spirit — the Comforter — was sent specifically to bring rest to your mind and peace to your spirit.

"Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest." — Matthew 11:28

Listen: The Comfort Ministry of the Holy Spirit Talk to Our Team
When You Feel Lost

You Have a Destiny. Discover It.

If you feel like you are wandering without purpose — God has a plan for your life that is greater than you can imagine. His Word is a lamp to your feet and a light to your path. The SOMI Church Mission exists to help you discover and walk in your Kingdom identity.

"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord — plans to prosper you and not harm you, to give you a hope and a future." — Jeremiah 29:11

Visit SOMI Church This Sunday Watch Sermons on Purpose
When You're Grieving

He Sees Your Tears

Loss is one of the deepest human pains. Whether you have lost a loved one, a relationship, a dream, or a season — Jesus wept. He understands grief. He sits with you in your sorrow and promises to turn your mourning into dancing.

"Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted." — Matthew 5:4

Request Prayer Support
When Your Faith Is Weak

The Fire Can Burn Again

Every believer goes through dry seasons. But the Holy Ghost is a consuming fire, and one encounter with the living God can reignite everything. The daily prayer watches and revelatory teachings at SOMI Church are designed to stoke your spiritual hunger.

"They who wait for the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings like eagles." — Isaiah 40:31
