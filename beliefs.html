What We Believe

Our Statement of Faith

Our beliefs are rooted in the eternal, unchanging Word of God. These are the truths that anchor every message, ministry, and moment at The SOMI Church Mission.

01

The Holy Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. It is the supreme standard for all faith and conduct. From Genesis to Revelation, every word is breathed by God and profitable for teaching, reproof, correction, and training in righteousness (2 Timothy 3:16–17).

02

The Triune God

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Each person is fully God, co-equal and co-eternal. This mystery of the Trinity is the foundation of our faith and the source of our life (Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14).

03

The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Jesus Christ — His virgin birth, sinless life, miraculous ministry, atoning death on the cross, bodily resurrection, ascension to the right hand of the Father, and His personal return in power and glory (John 1:1–14; Acts 1:11).

04

Salvation by Grace

We believe that salvation — the forgiveness of sins and eternal life — is the free gift of God through faith alone in Jesus Christ alone. It is not earned by human effort but received through repentance and trust in the finished work of Christ (Ephesians 2:8–9; Romans 10:9–10).

05

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present-day ministry of the Holy Spirit — the Third Person of the Trinity — who indwells every believer, empowers them for supernatural living, distributes spiritual gifts, and guides the Church into all truth. We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit with evidence of speaking in tongues (Acts 2:1–4; 1 Corinthians 12).

06

The Church

We believe in the universal Church — the body of Christ comprising all believers across the earth. The local church is God's primary vehicle for discipleship, worship, community, and mission. We are called to gather, grow, serve, and reach the lost together (Matthew 16:18; Hebrews 10:24–25).

07

Healing & Miracles

We believe in divine healing as provided in the atonement of Christ. God still heals, delivers, and performs miracles today. We pray for the sick, cast out demons in Jesus' name, and expect the supernatural power of God to be demonstrated in our midst (Isaiah 53:5; Mark 16:17–18; James 5:14–15).

08

The Second Coming of Christ

We believe in the personal, bodily return of Jesus Christ. He will come again in glory to judge the living and the dead. Every believer has the hope of resurrection and eternal life in the presence of God. This hope motivates everything we do (1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Revelation 22:20).
